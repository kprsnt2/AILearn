What you are looking at

Each cycle the swarm samples skills from the frontier of its own uncertainty, auctions a fixed escrow among candidate jobs, runs them, verdicts them with an independent oracle, and settles the stakes. A skill's authority is recomputed every cycle from its lower confidence bound, its evidence count, its age, and its observed cheating rate. Skills whose authority falls too far are demoted; skills that fail a re-test by more than the σ threshold are the most informative events in the whole simulation, because they are the ones that remove accumulated drift.

The divergence number is the fraction of claims that failed replication in the last three cycles — the same quantity the kill-switch uses. Watch what happens to it when you set decay to zero and drift to maximum: the system stays confident while the ground moves under it. That is the failure mode of every ledger-only agent system.

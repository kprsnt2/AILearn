Formula

bytes_per_token = 2 × L × Hkv × dhead × bytes
dhead = d_model / heads  ·  cache = 2 × L × Hkv × dhead × n_tokens × bytes

Prefill and decode are bandwidth-bound estimates: prefill ≈ (2 × params × prompt) / (bandwidth × efficiency), decode ≈ bandwidth / bytes_read_per_step. Real throughput depends on kernels, batching and hardware, so treat these as orders of magnitude, not benchmarks. The ratios between attention schemes are exact.

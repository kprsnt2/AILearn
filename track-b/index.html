TRACK B · SCALING & ARCHITECTURE

Scaling & Architecture - 25 lessons

Big-iron physics. Why DeepSeek made huge models affordable, on a napkin.

This is the track written for your blind spot, and it is the one that pays immediately. Every lesson turns an architecture choice into arithmetic you can do in a review: parameters, FLOPs, bytes, dollars, tokens per second. By the end you can look at any model announcement and independently reconstruct what it costs, why it was built that way, and what its serving economics must be.

L26 → L50 build: A cost model that prereq: Track A lessons 06, 09, 10 at minimum

Why this track exists

01

You will stop taking architecture claims on faith

Scaling laws, Chinchilla and the over-training reversal explain why models look the way they do in 2026.

02

DeepSeek's specific bets become legible

MLA, DeepSeekMoE, DSA, multi-token prediction and the V4.1 asymmetric encoder-decoder each solve one arithmetic problem.

03

Cost modelling becomes a deliverable

This is the single most valuable skill for someone applying for AI systems roles. Almost nobody can do it.

By the end you can

  • Derive the compute-optimal N and D for a given budget from a scaling law
  • Explain why inference volume moves the optimum away from Chinchilla
  • Compute the KV-cache bytes for MHA, GQA and MLA and pick one for a stated workload
  • Estimate a training run's GPU-hours and dollars with defensible assumptions

Prerequisites

Track A lessons 06, 09, 10 at minimum. Basic algebra. This track is arithmetic-heavy on purpose.

The artifact: A cost model that, given an architecture and a workload, predicts training cost, serving cost and the binding constraint.

TRACK A · FOUNDATIONS

Foundations - 25 lessons

The machine underneath. Build every block of a transformer yourself.

You can already orchestrate models. What you cannot yet do is rebuild one. This track closes that gap block by block: tokenizer, embedding, gradient, norm, attention, position, cache, sampler, full training loop. By lesson 25 you will have trained a small transformer from scratch and know exactly where every number in its loss curve came from.

L01 → L25 build: A working tiny transformer trained on a corpus prereq: Python, and a willingness to run things

Why this track exists

01

Every scaling decision becomes legible

You cannot judge MLA vs GQA vs MQA until you have computed a KV cache by hand.

02

Debugging stops being guessing

Most 'the model is broken' bugs are tensor-shape or masking bugs. You will have made all of them yourself in a sandbox.

03

Cost intuition becomes arithmetic

Token counts, cache bytes, FLOPs. You will be able to estimate a serving bill on a napkin.

By the end you can

  • Explain the variance argument for dividing attention by sqrt(d_k) without notes
  • Compute KV-cache bytes for any config and say which tensor dominates at 128k context
  • Get a clean training run with an LR schedule and no loss spikes
  • Read a model repo (nanoGPT / HF) and know what every line does

Prerequisites

Python, and a willingness to run things. No deep-learning maths assumed.

The artifact: A working tiny transformer trained on a corpus, with a hand-written sampler, attention and training loop.
